PageWright Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version

PageWright does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you.

What stays on your computer

Everything you write, the pictures you add, your styles and your settings are saved only on your own computer. PageWright does not upload them anywhere. Files you export go only where you choose to save or send them.

What PageWright does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No crash reports or usage data sent to us.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.

Purchases

Purchases and the free trial are handled by the app store you bought PageWright from. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The store's own privacy policy applies to those purchases.

Children

PageWright collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
